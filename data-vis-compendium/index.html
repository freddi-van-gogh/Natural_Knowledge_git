How this was made

The ideas, the questions, and the decisions in this compendium are the author's. The author set out what it should show and in what order, and chose the stops and side roads. AI assistants turned that into pages, checked it against sources, and raised questions where the notes were wrong or unclear. The author answered them, made editorial calls, and drafted some text sections themselves.

In the terms publishers now use, the text is AI-generated: an AI drafted the bulk of the compendium alongside author inputs and rewrites, working from the author's notes and choices and from sources that AI research assistants gathered and checked. The animations, simulations, and drawings were also made with an AI assistant, in code. The videos and pictures by others are credited to them.
